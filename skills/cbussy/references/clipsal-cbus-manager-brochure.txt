Pair Controller

13. Reboot the controller.
14. Once the controller has successfully rebooted, refresh the web browser*.
15. Navigate to the Management page – Cloud Access tab, and click Pair Controller.




*Pairing is possible only in the first five minutes after the device boots up. Restart your device (using a physical restart button) if needed.

accessed via the Clipsal C-Bus Manager smart phone application:

1. Open the Clipsal C-Bus Manager application and login to the same account that was used in the controller (Page 8) .
